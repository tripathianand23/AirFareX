AIRFAREX INDIA ROUTE MAP — GEOGRAPHIC DATA ATTRIBUTION

Official outer outline:
Survey of India, International Boundary of India, 1:16 million vector.
Source: https://surveyofindia.gov.in/
The SOI-derived outline is used as the India-only outer geography.
Public redistribution/publication must comply with SOI licence terms.

Approximate Line of Control:
UN Geospatial boundary data, feature classified as a more-dotted
boundary line (IND_PAK). Displayed separately and labelled approximate.
It is not represented as an international boundary.

Internal state and UT boundaries:
Natural Earth Admin-1, India features, clipped to the SOI outline.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

River centerlines:
Natural Earth Rivers + Lake Centerlines, clipped to the SOI outline.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/

Airport coordinates:
OurAirports public airport data.
https://ourairports.com/data/

Route lines:
Schematic airport-to-airport connections generated from AirFareX
monitored route records. They are not actual flight trajectories.
